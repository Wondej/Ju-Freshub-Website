Frequently asked questions

Is JU FreshHub free?

You can start free and use many features. Pro unlocks all exams, videos and extra tools.

Which devices does it work on?

The app is for Android phones. Use the download button to get it.

Which subjects are included?

Mathematics, General Physics, Logic, Geography, English and Psychology, and more are being added.

Can I study without internet?

Yes. Saved content and downloaded study material can be opened offline. Videos need internet because they play from YouTube.

Are explanations in Amharic?

Many questions have both English and Amharic explanations so you can choose.

How do I unlock Pro?

Pay, upload a clear screenshot of your real receipt in the app, and wait for it to be checked. Contact us if it takes long.
